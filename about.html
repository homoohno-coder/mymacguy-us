My Background

15+ Years of Apple & Business IT Experience

Wahed Tech Support is a sole proprietorship owned and operated by Wahed Khaliqi. It provides independent remote support and on-site service at client locations throughout Greater Los Angeles. The business address is not a storefront; device drop-off and pickup are available by prior appointment only, with no walk-ins.

I'm Wahed, and I've spent more than 15 years working hands-on with Apple devices, business networks, and everyday office technology. Before founding this practice, I ran IT operations for a major Los Angeles talent agency — supporting agents, executives, and staff who depended on their technology working every single day.

That experience shaped how I work now: I don't treat clients like tickets in a queue. I learn each business — its people, its devices, its workflow — so that when something comes up, I already know the context.

Today I bring that same level of care to talent agencies, production companies, art studios, law firms, and other small professional teams across Los Angeles.
