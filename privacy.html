Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Wahed Tech Support operates mymacguy.us. This page explains what information the site collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you. Data collection is kept to the minimum needed to respond to your enquiry and provide IT support.

Information Collected on This Website

Nothing is required to browse this site. You can read every page without creating an account or entering personal details.

  • Contact you initiate. If you call, text, email, or submit the contact form, we receive whatever you choose to share: your name, company, phone number, email address, and a description of your IT needs.
  • Standard hosting logs. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare. Those providers may record technical data such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested, under their own privacy policies.
  • Embedded map. The contact page embeds a Google Map. Loading it may allow Google to collect data in accordance with Google's privacy policy.

How Information Is Used

  • To respond to your enquiry and schedule support.
  • To assess, document, and resolve the technical issue you reported.
  • To keep a service history so future support is faster and better informed.
  • To send follow-up messages directly related to work performed.

What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
  • We do not run advertising networks or behavioral ad tracking on this site.
  • We do not add you to marketing lists without you asking.
  • We do not request or store your passwords. You enter your own credentials yourself during any support session.

Data Accessed During a Support Engagement

Providing IT support sometimes means accessing business systems: email accounts, Microsoft 365 administration, device settings, network configuration, or backup systems. That access is used only to complete the requested work, is never copied for any other purpose, and is treated as strictly confidential. Remote sessions are conducted with your consent and end when the session closes.

Retention

Service notes and contact details are kept for as long as needed to support you and to meet ordinary business and tax record requirements. You may request deletion of your records at any time.

Your Choices and Rights

You may ask what information we hold about you, request corrections, request deletion, or ask us to stop contacting you. California residents may have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, including the right to know and the right to delete. To make a request, email wahed@mymacguy.us.

Children

This site is intended for businesses arranging IT services and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

This site is served over HTTPS. Reasonable measures are taken to protect information, though no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Third-Party Links

Pages may link to third parties such as technology partners or vendor support articles. Their privacy practices are their own and are not covered by this policy.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The revision date at the top will change when it does.

Contact

Questions about privacy: wahed@mymacguy.us or (310) 980-1964, Los Angeles, California.
