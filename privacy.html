Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 August 2026

Wahed Tech Support operates mymacguy.us. This page explains what information the site collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to answer your enquiry and provide technical support.

Information collected on this website

  • Nothing is required to browse. You can read every page, including the self-help troubleshooter, without creating an account or entering personal details.
  • Self-help troubleshooter input. What you type into the troubleshooter is processed entirely inside your own browser to select the matching guidance. It is not transmitted to us or stored on a server.
  • Contact you initiate. If you call, text or email using the links provided, we receive whatever you choose to share: your name, phone number, email address and a description of your technical problem.
  • Standard hosting logs. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare. Those providers may record technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages requested for security and reliability purposes, under their own privacy policies.
  • Embedded map. The contact section embeds a Google Map. Loading it may allow Google to collect data in accordance with Google's privacy policy.

How information is used

  • To respond to your enquiry and schedule support.
  • To diagnose, document and resolve the technical issue you reported.
  • To keep a service history so that future visits are faster and better informed.
  • To send follow-up messages directly related to work performed.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.
  • We do not run advertising networks or behavioural ad tracking on this site.
  • We do not add you to marketing lists without you asking.
  • We do not request or store your passwords. During a support session you enter your own credentials yourself.

Data collected during a support visit

Providing technical support sometimes means seeing the contents of a device: file names, photos, email, account settings and error logs. That access is used only to complete the requested repair or configuration, is never copied for any other purpose, and is treated as strictly confidential. Remote sessions are conducted with your consent and end when the session closes.

Retention

Service notes and contact details are kept for as long as needed to support you and to meet ordinary business and tax record requirements. You may request deletion of your records at any time.

Your choices and rights

You may ask what information we hold about you, request corrections, request deletion, or ask us to stop contacting you. California residents may have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, including the right to know and the right to delete. To make a request, email wahed@mymacguy.us.

Children

This site is intended for adults arranging technical services and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

This site is served over HTTPS. Reasonable measures are taken to protect information, though no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Third-party links

Pages may link to third parties such as hardware partners or manufacturer support articles. Their privacy practices are their own and are not covered by this policy.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The revision date at the top will change when it does.

Contact
